# 13. Questions YOU Must Ask the HR / Recruiter



### Must Know

1. "What does success look like for this role in the first 90 days?"
2. "How would you describe the engineering culture and work-life balance on this team?"
3. "What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?"
4. "What are the opportunities for mentorship, learning, and career advancement?"
5. "What are the next steps in the interview process and the expected timeline?"
