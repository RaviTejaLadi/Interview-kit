# 9. Mentorship, Cross-Functional Collaboration & Leadership



### Must Know

1. Tell me about a time you mentored or onboarded a junior engineer or intern.
2. How do you collaborate with non-technical stakeholders (Product Managers, Designers, QA, Sales)?
3. How do you explain complex technical concepts to non-technical team members or clients?
4. Tell me about a time you took the lead on an initiative without being formally asked.
5. How do you promote knowledge sharing within your team?
