# 10. Ambiguity & Fast-Paced Change

### Must Know

1. Tell me about a time requirements changed halfway through a project. How did you adapt?
2. How do you approach a task when the requirements are vague or undefined?
3. Tell me about a time you had to learn a completely new technology or framework on a tight deadline.
4. Have you ever worked in a startup or fast-paced environment where processes were informal?
