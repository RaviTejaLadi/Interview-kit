# How do you choose the critical path to automate?

**Definition:**
Pick journeys that make money or block all users: sign up/login, core search, add to cart, checkout, or 'create document and share'. Skip pixel-perfect settings screens. Ask: if this broke at 2am, would we page? Those are E2E. Everything else moves down the pyramid.

**Key points:**
- Revenue and login first.
- One path per product area, not every permutation.
- Data setup must be reliable.
- Owner team for failures.
- Map to SLOs.
