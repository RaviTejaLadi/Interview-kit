# What is a skill you struggled to master and how did you overcome it?

### Definition

This question looks for **persistence and learning ability**.

The interviewer wants to understand your learning process.

A strong answer explains:

1. What was difficult
2. Why it was difficult
3. What you did
4. How you practiced
5. What changed

### Sample answer

> “One area I initially struggled with was understanding JavaScript's asynchronous behavior and the event loop.
>
> I could use Promises and async/await, but I didn't initially have a strong mental model of how the event loop, microtask queue, timers, and asynchronous APIs interacted.
>
> Instead of just memorizing behavior, I started creating small examples and predicting the execution order before running them. I also studied how the browser and Node.js handle asynchronous operations.
>
> After practicing this way, I became much more comfortable debugging issues involving Promises, timers, async operations, and race conditions.
>
> The experience also taught me that when a concept is difficult, building a small mental model and experimenting with it is more effective for me than simply reading documentation repeatedly.”
