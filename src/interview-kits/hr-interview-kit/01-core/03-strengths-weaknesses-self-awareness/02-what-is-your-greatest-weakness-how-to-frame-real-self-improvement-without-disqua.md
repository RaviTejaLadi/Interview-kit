# What is your greatest weakness? (How to frame real self-improvement without disqualifying yourself).

### Definition

This question tests **self-awareness and your ability to improve**.

A good weakness should be:

- Real
- Professional
- Something you can improve
- Not a core requirement that would disqualify you
- Accompanied by a concrete improvement strategy

Avoid fake weaknesses such as:

> “I'm a perfectionist.”

or:

> “I work too hard.”

These sound rehearsed.

### Sample answer

> “One area I've been working on is spending too much time evaluating different implementation approaches before committing to one.
>
> As a developer, I like understanding the trade-offs, but I've learned that spending too much time optimizing a decision that has relatively low impact can slow down development.
>
> I've been improving this by identifying which decisions are reversible and which are architectural. For smaller decisions, I make a reasonable choice quickly and move forward. For decisions with long-term impact, I spend more time evaluating the trade-offs.
>
> That has helped me become more decisive without sacrificing engineering quality.”

### Why this works

It shows:

**Weakness → Awareness → Action → Improvement**
