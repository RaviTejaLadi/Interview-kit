# What is one area where you received constructive feedback, and how did you act on it?

### Definition

This question checks whether you can:

- Accept feedback
- Avoid becoming defensive
- Convert feedback into action
- Demonstrate improvement

### Sample answer

> “One piece of feedback I received was that I sometimes focused heavily on the technical implementation before communicating the approach to the wider team.
>
> I realized that even if the implementation is technically good, other engineers and stakeholders need to understand the approach and potential trade-offs.
>
> I started discussing the approach earlier, especially for larger features. For complex changes, I now share a short technical explanation or discuss the proposed architecture before starting implementation.
>
> That has improved collaboration and also helped identify potential issues earlier.”

### Strong interview pattern

```text
Feedback
   ↓
I understood the reason
   ↓
I changed my behavior
   ↓
Result
```

Don't say:

> “My manager was wrong, but I followed the feedback.”

That defeats the purpose of the question.
