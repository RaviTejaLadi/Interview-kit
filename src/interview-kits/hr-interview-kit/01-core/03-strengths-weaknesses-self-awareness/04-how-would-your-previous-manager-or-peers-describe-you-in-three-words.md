# How would your previous manager or peers describe you in three words?

### Definition

This question checks whether you understand **how others perceive your working style**.

Choose three characteristics that you can actually support with examples.

For a software engineer, good options include:

- Reliable
- Curious
- Analytical
- Collaborative
- Proactive
- Detail-oriented
- Adaptable
- Dependable
- Ownership-driven

### Sample answer

> “I think they would describe me as **reliable, analytical, and collaborative**.
>
> Reliable because I take ownership of the tasks I commit to and communicate early when I see a risk.
>
> Analytical because I usually try to understand the root cause of a technical problem rather than immediately applying a workaround.
>
> Collaborative because I enjoy discussing technical approaches with other developers and working closely with backend, design, and product teams.”

### Important

Don't just say:

> “Hardworking, passionate, dedicated.”

Those are generic unless you explain them.
