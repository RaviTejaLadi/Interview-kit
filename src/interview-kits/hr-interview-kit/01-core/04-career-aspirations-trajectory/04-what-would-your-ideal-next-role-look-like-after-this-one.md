# What would your ideal next role look like after this one?

### Definition

This question evaluates whether you have a **logical career progression**.

The interviewer wants to know what you expect to gain from the current role and what you want to eventually grow into.

### Sample answer

> “After this role, I would ideally like to move into a position where I have broader technical ownership.
>
> I would like to work on larger frontend systems, participate more actively in architecture and system-design discussions, and own technical initiatives from design through implementation.
>
> I'd also like to spend more time mentoring engineers and improving engineering practices across the team.
>
> The important thing for me is that the next role should represent an increase in scope and impact rather than simply a change in job title.”

### Why this answer works

You're defining progression as:

```text
Current role
     ↓
More complex problems
     ↓
More ownership
     ↓
Broader technical influence
     ↓
Greater impact
```
