# Do you see yourself moving toward individual contributor (IC / Staff Engineer) or engineering management?

### Definition

This question asks about your **career track preference**.

There are generally two paths:

### Individual Contributor

```text
Engineer
   ↓
Senior Engineer
   ↓
Staff Engineer
   ↓
Principal Engineer
```

The focus is usually:

- Technical architecture
- Complex problems
- System design
- Technical leadership
- Mentoring
- Engineering standards

### Engineering Management

```text
Engineer
   ↓
Tech Lead / Manager
   ↓
Engineering Manager
   ↓
Senior Engineering Manager
```

The focus is usually:

- People management
- Hiring
- Team development
- Planning
- Delivery
- Performance management
- Organizational leadership

### Sample answer — IC direction

> “At this stage, I'm more interested in the individual contributor path.
>
> I enjoy solving technical problems, understanding system architecture, and going deep into frontend engineering. I also enjoy mentoring other developers and contributing to technical decisions.
>
> Over time, I'd like to grow toward a senior or staff-level engineering role where I can influence architecture and engineering practices across larger areas of the product.
>
> I'm not completely ruling out management in the future, but my current focus is becoming a stronger technical individual contributor.”

### If you're undecided

> “At this stage, I'm leaning toward the IC path because I enjoy technical problem-solving and architecture. I also enjoy mentoring and collaboration, so I want to develop those leadership skills as well. As my career progresses, I can make a more informed decision about whether formal people management is the right direction.”

That's completely reasonable.
