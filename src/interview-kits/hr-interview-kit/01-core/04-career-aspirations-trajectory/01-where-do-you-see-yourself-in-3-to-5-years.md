# Where do you see yourself in 3 to 5 years?

### Definition

This question evaluates your **career direction and professional ambition**.

A good answer should show:

- Growth in technical responsibility
- Increasing ownership
- Deeper expertise
- Ability to work on larger systems
- Potential leadership

You don't need to predict your exact job title.

### Sample answer

> “Over the next three to five years, I want to grow into a senior-level frontend engineer who can take ownership of larger and more complex systems.
>
> I want to become stronger in frontend architecture, performance, scalability, and technical decision-making rather than focusing only on feature implementation.
>
> I also want to contribute beyond my own tasks by helping other engineers, participating in architecture discussions, improving development practices, and taking ownership of important technical initiatives.
>
> Ideally, I want to be someone the team can rely on for complex frontend problems and architectural decisions.”

### Strong idea

Think:

**More responsibility → more technical depth → more impact**

rather than:

> “I want to become a manager in three years.”
