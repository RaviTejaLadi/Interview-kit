# What are your long-term career goals?

### Definition

This question asks about your **broader professional objectives**, beyond your next job.

Good goals for a frontend engineer can include:

- Becoming an expert in frontend architecture
- Leading technical initiatives
- Designing scalable systems
- Mentoring engineers
- Contributing to engineering standards
- Improving developer experience
- Building high-quality products

### Sample answer

> “My long-term goal is to become a strong technical leader in frontend engineering.
>
> I want to develop deep expertise in areas such as React architecture, TypeScript, web performance, accessibility, frontend system design, and scalable application architecture.
>
> Beyond my own technical work, I want to contribute to engineering standards, mentor other developers, and help teams make better technical decisions.
>
> Ultimately, I want my impact to extend beyond the code I personally write.”

### Key phrase

> **“I want my impact to extend beyond the code I personally write.”**

That's particularly useful when discussing senior/staff-level ambitions.
