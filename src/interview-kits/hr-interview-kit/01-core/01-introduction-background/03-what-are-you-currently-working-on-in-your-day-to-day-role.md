# What are you currently working on in your day-to-day role?

### Definition

This question focuses on your **current responsibilities and technical contribution**.

A strong answer should explain:

1. What product/application you work on
2. Your role in the product
3. Technologies you use
4. Typical tasks
5. Technical challenges
6. Your impact

### Sample answer

> “Currently, I work primarily on a React-based application.
>
> My day-to-day work involves developing new features, building reusable UI components, integrating REST APIs, handling application state, fixing bugs, and improving the performance and maintainability of the frontend.
>
> I also work closely with backend developers, designers, and product teams to understand requirements and convert them into technical implementations.
>
> From a technical perspective, I spend a lot of time thinking about component architecture, state management, API data flow, error handling, responsive design, and reusable patterns.
>
> Apart from feature development, I also participate in code reviews, debugging production issues, and improving existing code.”

### Stronger version

Instead of saying:

> “I work on React and build UI.”

Say:

> “I work on a React-based application where I’m responsible for implementing features, designing reusable components, integrating APIs, managing frontend state, and solving performance and maintainability issues.”

That gives the interviewer much more information.
