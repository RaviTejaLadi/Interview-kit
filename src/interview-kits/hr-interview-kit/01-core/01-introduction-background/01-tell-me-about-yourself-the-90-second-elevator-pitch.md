# "Tell me about yourself." (The 90-second elevator pitch).

### Definition

This is a short professional introduction that summarizes:

- Who you are
- Your years/type of experience
- Your primary technical skills
- What you currently work on
- A couple of important achievements
- What kind of role you are looking for

The goal is **not to explain your entire resume**. It should give the interviewer a clear picture of your professional background in about **60–90 seconds**.

### Sample answer

> “Hi, I’m Ravi, a software developer specializing in frontend development with a strong focus on React.js and modern JavaScript.
>
> In my current role, I work on building and maintaining web applications, mainly focusing on reusable React components, state management, API integration, performance, and responsive UI development.
>
> Over time, I’ve worked with technologies such as React, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, CSS, Git, and Node.js. I’ve also spent a lot of time understanding frontend architecture rather than just implementing individual UI screens.
>
> One area I particularly enjoy is building reusable and scalable components and solving complex UI and data-flow problems.
>
> I’m now looking for an opportunity where I can work on larger-scale applications, contribute to technical decisions, and continue growing as a frontend engineer.”

**Tip:** Don't start with personal details such as your hometown, age, family, or hobbies unless the interviewer specifically asks.
