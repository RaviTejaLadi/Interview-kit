# Walk me through your resume.

### Definition

This question asks you to **explain your career journey in chronological or logical order**.

The interviewer wants to understand:

- Your education/background
- How you entered software development
- Your previous companies/roles
- Major responsibilities
- Important projects
- Technology progression
- Why you moved between roles

Think of your resume as a **story**, not a list of bullet points.

### Recommended structure

```text
Education / Starting point
        ↓
First software role
        ↓
Important responsibilities
        ↓
Major projects
        ↓
Skills gained
        ↓
Current role
        ↓
Current responsibilities
        ↓
Next career goal
```

### Sample answer

> “Sure. I started my career with a strong interest in web development and gradually moved into frontend engineering.
>
> In my earlier role, I focused mainly on HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and building UI features. That gave me a good foundation in browser fundamentals and frontend development.
>
> As I gained experience, I moved into React.js and started working on larger applications involving reusable components, API integration, state management, and more complex UI requirements.
>
> In my current role, my responsibilities are more focused on building scalable React applications, designing reusable components, integrating backend APIs, debugging production issues, and improving application performance.
>
> One major progression in my career has been moving from simply implementing UI requirements to thinking more about component architecture, maintainability, performance, and the overall frontend system.”

### Interview tip

Don't read your resume line by line.

Instead, explain **how one experience led to the next**.
