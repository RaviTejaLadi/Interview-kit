# 2. Motivation & Company Fit

### Must Know

1. Why do you want to work at our company?
2. What do you know about our product, culture, and business model?
3. Why are you looking to leave your current job?
4. What didn't you like about your previous or current job? (Diplomacy test).
5. Why should we hire you over other candidates?
6. What kind of work environment or company culture allows you to do your best work?
7. How does this role align with your personal values?
