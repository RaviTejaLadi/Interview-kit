# How do you manage your time when working remotely or in a hybrid setup?

### Definition

This question checks your ability to maintain **productivity and communication without constant supervision**.

The interviewer wants to know whether you can manage your own work while staying connected with the team.

### Sample answer

> “I manage remote work by starting with clear priorities for the day and breaking larger tasks into smaller deliverables.
>
> I usually identify the most important engineering task first and allocate focused time for it. I also keep my task status updated so that the team knows what I'm working on and whether anything is blocked.
>
> For communication, I try to be proactive rather than waiting for someone to ask for an update. If I encounter a blocker, I communicate it early.
>
> I also try to keep meetings and collaboration separate from focused development time where possible.
>
> The main thing for me is maintaining both productivity and visibility. Remote work shouldn't mean working in isolation.”
