# Do you prefer working independently or in close pair-programming collaboration?

### Definition

This question evaluates your **collaboration style and adaptability**.

There isn't one universally correct answer.

A good engineer should be able to:

- Work independently
- Collaborate when needed
- Ask for help
- Help others
- Choose the appropriate approach based on the problem

### Sample answer

> “I’m comfortable with both.
>
> For well-defined tasks, I generally prefer working independently because it allows me to maintain focus and move efficiently.
>
> For complex problems, architectural decisions, difficult bugs, or unfamiliar areas of the codebase, I find collaboration very useful. Pair programming can be especially effective when two people bring different perspectives to the problem.
>
> I don't see independent work and collaboration as opposites. I prefer choosing the approach based on the complexity and nature of the task.
>
> For example, I might investigate a problem independently first and then pair with another engineer once I have enough context to have a productive technical discussion.”
