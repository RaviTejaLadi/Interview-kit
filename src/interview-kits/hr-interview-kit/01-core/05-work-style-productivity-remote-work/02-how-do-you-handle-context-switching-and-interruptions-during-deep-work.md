# How do you handle context-switching and interruptions during deep work?

### Definition

**Context-switching** means repeatedly moving between different tasks, such as coding, Slack messages, meetings, debugging, and code reviews.

Too much context-switching can reduce concentration and increase mistakes.

### Sample answer

> “I try to protect blocks of uninterrupted time for complex development work.
>
> When I'm working on something that requires deep concentration, I avoid constantly switching between the task and non-critical notifications. I usually group smaller activities such as code reviews, messages, and documentation into specific periods.
>
> At the same time, I don't completely ignore interruptions. If something is genuinely urgent, especially a production issue or a blocker for another developer, I'll handle it.
>
> After an interruption, I usually leave myself a short note about where I stopped and what I was thinking about. That makes it easier to resume the task without spending a lot of time reconstructing the context.”

### Good principle

> **Protect deep work, but remain available for genuinely important issues.**
