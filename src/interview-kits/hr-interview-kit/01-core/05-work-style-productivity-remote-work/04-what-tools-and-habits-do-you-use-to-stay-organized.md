# What tools and habits do you use to stay organized?

### Definition

This question explores your **personal productivity system**.

The specific tools matter less than whether you have a reliable process.

Common tools include:

- Jira / Linear
- GitHub
- Slack / Teams
- Calendar
- Notion / Confluence
- IDE task lists
- Personal notes

### Sample answer

> “I usually rely on the team's project-management tool for tracking tasks and a calendar for meetings and important deadlines.
>
> For development work, I break larger tasks into smaller steps so that I can track progress and identify blockers early.
>
> I also keep short technical notes for complex features, especially when there are important decisions or assumptions that I may need to revisit later.
>
> One habit I find useful is ending the day by checking what was completed, what is still pending, and what I should prioritize next. That gives me a clear starting point the following day.
>
> I try to keep the system simple. The goal is to reduce mental overhead rather than create another process to maintain.”

### Strong point

> **Tools support the process; they shouldn't become the process.**
