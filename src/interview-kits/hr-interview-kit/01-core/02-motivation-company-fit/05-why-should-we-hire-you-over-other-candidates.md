# Why should we hire you over other candidates?

### Definition

This question is asking you to explain your **value proposition**.

Don't compare yourself directly with candidates you don't know.

Instead, explain:

**Your relevant skills + experience + working style + evidence**

### Sample answer

> “I think my experience aligns well with this role because I have strong hands-on experience with React and modern frontend development.
>
> Beyond building UI, I focus on component architecture, reusable patterns, API integration, state management, performance, and maintainability.
>
> I also try to understand the problem behind a requirement rather than simply implementing the ticket. That helps me make better technical decisions and identify potential issues early.
>
> Another strength I bring is that I'm comfortable working across the development lifecycle — understanding requirements, implementing features, debugging issues, reviewing code, and collaborating with backend and product teams.
>
> Based on the requirements of this role, I believe those skills would allow me to contribute effectively.”

### Strong formula

```text
Relevant experience
        +
Technical strengths
        +
Problem-solving ability
        +
Ownership
        +
Evidence
```

Don't say:

> “I'm better than the other candidates.”

You don't know the other candidates.
