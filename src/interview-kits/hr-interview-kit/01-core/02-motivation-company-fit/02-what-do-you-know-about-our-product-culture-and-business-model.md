# What do you know about our product, culture, and business model?

### Definition

This checks whether you have **done your research before the interview**.

You should understand three things:

- **Product** → What does the company build?
- **Business model** → How does the company make money?
- **Culture** → How does the company describe its working principles?

### Sample answer

> “From my research, I understand that your company provides [product/service] primarily for [target customers].
>
> The main problem the product solves is [problem], and the company generates revenue through [subscription/SaaS/licensing/marketplace/etc.].
>
> From the engineering and company information I've seen, some of the cultural themes that stood out to me are [ownership/customer focus/collaboration/technical excellence].
>
> From a frontend perspective, I find the product particularly interesting because [specific technical/product reason]. That's one of the reasons this role caught my attention.”

### 💡 Interview preparation

Before the interview, research:

```text
Company website
    ↓
Product
    ↓
Customers
    ↓
Business model
    ↓
Competitors
    ↓
Engineering blog
    ↓
Company values
    ↓
Recent product updates
```

The more specific your answer, the more genuine it sounds.
