# What kind of work environment or company culture allows you to do your best work?

### Definition

This question checks whether your **working preferences match the team's environment**.

Think about:

- Collaboration
- Communication
- Autonomy
- Code reviews
- Documentation
- Technical discussions
- Feedback
- Ownership
- Learning

### Sample answer

> “I work best in an environment where engineers have ownership of their work but also collaborate closely with the team.
>
> I appreciate teams where technical decisions are discussed openly, code reviews are constructive, and people are comfortable asking questions or challenging an approach respectfully.
>
> I also prefer an environment where requirements and priorities are communicated clearly, while engineers still have enough autonomy to decide how to implement a solution.
>
> For me, a good engineering culture is one where people care about both delivering features and maintaining the quality of the codebase.”

### Good phrase

> **“High ownership with healthy collaboration.”**

That's a strong way to summarize your preference.
