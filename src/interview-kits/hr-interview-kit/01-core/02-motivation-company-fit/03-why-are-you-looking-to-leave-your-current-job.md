# Why are you looking to leave your current job?

### Definition

This question explores your **motivation for changing jobs**.

The safest approach is to focus on **what you want next**, rather than criticizing your current employer.

### Sample answer

> “I've learned a lot in my current role and had the opportunity to work on several interesting projects.
>
> At this stage, I'm looking for an opportunity where I can take on more ownership, work on larger-scale frontend systems, and contribute more to technical decisions.
>
> I'm particularly interested in roles where I can work deeply with React, TypeScript, frontend architecture, performance, and reusable component systems.
>
> So my decision is mainly driven by growth and the type of technical challenges I want to take on next.”

### Good framing

**Current job gave me X → I learned Y → Now I want Z.**
