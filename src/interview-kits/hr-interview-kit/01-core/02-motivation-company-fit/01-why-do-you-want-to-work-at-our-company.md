# Why do you want to work at our company?

### Definition

This question tests whether you have a **specific reason for applying** rather than applying randomly.

A strong answer connects:

**Company → Role → Your skills → Career growth**

### Sample answer

> “I’m interested in this company because the product and the engineering challenges are closely aligned with my experience in frontend development.
>
> I’m particularly interested in the opportunity to work on large-scale React applications and contribute to building reusable, maintainable frontend systems.
>
> I also see this role as an opportunity to work with experienced engineers, take ownership of larger features, and grow beyond just implementing UI requirements.
>
> So for me, the combination of the product, technical challenges, and the responsibilities of this role makes the opportunity interesting.”

### Avoid

> “I want to join because your company is a market leader and has a great reputation.”

That's too generic.
