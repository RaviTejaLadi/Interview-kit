# Tell me about a time you had to work with someone difficult or abrasive.

**Definition:**  
This question evaluates your emotional maturity and ability to maintain professional relationships under pressure.

**Sample answer:**

> I once worked with a teammate who communicated very directly during discussions and code reviews. Initially, some of the comments felt quite harsh.
>
> Instead of responding emotionally, I tried to understand whether there was a technical concern behind the communication. I also had a private conversation with them and explained that I was comfortable receiving critical feedback, but I preferred feedback to focus on the implementation rather than the person.
>
> After that conversation, our communication improved. I also made sure to keep my own communication professional and focused on solving the problem.
>
> The experience taught me that difficult communication doesn't always mean difficult intentions. Sometimes setting clear communication expectations can solve the problem.

**Avoid saying:**

> "They were terrible to work with."

Instead, describe the **behavior**, its **impact**, and how you handled it.
