# How do you deal with a colleague who isn't pulling their weight or missing deadlines?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer is checking whether you address performance issues directly and constructively instead of immediately blaming or escalating the person.

**Sample answer:**

> First, I try to understand why the deadline is being missed. There could be blockers, unclear requirements, unrealistic estimates, or dependencies that I'm not aware of.
>
> I would have a private conversation with the teammate and ask if there is anything blocking them. If I can help with something, I offer support.
>
> If the problem continues, I make the impact visible to the team or manager, especially if it affects the overall delivery.
>
> For example, instead of saying, "You're not completing your work," I would say, "This task is blocking the API integration, and we're now at risk of missing the testing deadline. Is there anything we can do to unblock it?"
>
> My goal is to solve the delivery problem rather than assign blame.

**Key principle:**

> **Understand → Support → Communicate impact → Escalate when necessary**
