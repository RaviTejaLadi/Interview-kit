# Tell me about a time you had a technical disagreement with a teammate or senior engineer. How was it resolved?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to know how you handle technical disagreements without turning them into personal conflicts. Focus on evidence, trade-offs, and the final team decision.

**Sample answer:**

> In one project, I had a disagreement with a senior engineer about how we should manage state in a React feature. They preferred keeping most of the state in a global store, while I felt that some of it was local UI state and didn't need to be global.
>
> Instead of debating based on personal preference, I explained my reasoning and compared both approaches based on maintainability, component reusability, and unnecessary re-renders.
>
> We created a small proof of concept and measured the complexity of both approaches. After discussing the results, we agreed to keep server-related state and shared application state centralized, while keeping temporary UI state local.
>
> The important part was that we focused on the technical trade-offs rather than who was right.

**What this demonstrates:**  
`Technical reasoning → evidence → collaboration → compromise`
