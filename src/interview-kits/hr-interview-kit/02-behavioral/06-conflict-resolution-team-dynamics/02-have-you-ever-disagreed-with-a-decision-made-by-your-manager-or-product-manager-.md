# Have you ever disagreed with a decision made by your manager or product manager? What did you do?

**Definition:**  
This tests whether you can disagree professionally while still respecting the final decision and business priorities.

**Sample answer:**

> Yes. In one project, the product team wanted to release a feature quickly with a particular implementation. I was concerned that the approach would create technical debt and make future changes more difficult.
>
> I explained my concerns to the manager and product manager, but I also understood that the release had a business deadline.
>
> We discussed the trade-off and decided to proceed with a simpler implementation for the initial release. I suggested documenting the technical limitation and creating a follow-up task to improve the implementation later.
>
> Once the decision was made, I supported it and focused on delivering the feature properly within the agreed scope.
>
> I think it's important to separate **disagreeing with a decision** from **disagreeing with the person making the decision**.

**Good interview phrase:**

> "I make sure my concerns are heard, but once the decision is made, I commit to it unless there is a significant technical or business risk."
