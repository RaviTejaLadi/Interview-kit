# Describe a time you felt overwhelmed at work. How did you cope?

**Definition:**  
This evaluates how you manage workload and pressure. Show that you can recognize when you're overloaded, prioritize, communicate, and maintain quality.

**Sample answer:**

> There was a period when I had several feature tasks, production fixes, and code reviews competing for my attention at the same time. Initially, I tried to handle everything simultaneously, but I realized that context-switching was reducing my productivity.
>
> I stepped back and categorized the work based on urgency and impact. I handled the production issue first, then focused on the highest-priority feature and scheduled time for the remaining work.
>
> I also communicated with my manager about the workload and clarified which tasks could be moved to a later sprint.
>
> After that, I started using a more structured approach to prioritization and avoided switching between unrelated tasks unnecessarily.
>
> The experience taught me that when the workload becomes overwhelming, the solution isn't simply to work longer. It's to **prioritize, communicate, and focus on one important thing at a time**.
