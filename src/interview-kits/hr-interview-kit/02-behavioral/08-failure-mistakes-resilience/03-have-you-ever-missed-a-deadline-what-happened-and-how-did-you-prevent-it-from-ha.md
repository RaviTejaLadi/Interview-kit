# Have you ever missed a deadline? What happened and how did you prevent it from happening again?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer isn't necessarily looking for someone who has never missed a deadline. They want to know whether you **communicate risks early and learn from inaccurate estimates**.

**Sample answer:**

> Yes. I once underestimated the amount of work required for a frontend feature because I focused mainly on the UI implementation and didn't account enough for API integration, loading states, error handling, and edge cases.
>
> As I got closer to the deadline, I realized the original estimate wasn't realistic. I communicated the risk to my manager instead of waiting until the deadline.
>
> We prioritized the core functionality and moved some non-critical improvements to the following sprint.
>
> After that experience, I started breaking larger tasks into smaller pieces and explicitly considering integration, testing, edge cases, and dependencies when estimating.
>
> I also became more comfortable communicating early when an estimate was at risk rather than trying to hide the problem until the last moment.

**Strong interview line:**

> "The biggest improvement wasn't learning to work faster; it was learning to estimate the complete scope more accurately."
