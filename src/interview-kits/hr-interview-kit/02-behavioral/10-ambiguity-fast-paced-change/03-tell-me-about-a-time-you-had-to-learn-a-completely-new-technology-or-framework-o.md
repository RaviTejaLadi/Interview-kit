# Tell me about a time you had to learn a completely new technology or framework on a tight deadline.

**Definition:**  
This evaluates your **learning ability, prioritization, and practical problem-solving**. They aren't necessarily looking for someone who knows every technology. They want to see how you learn when you need to deliver.

**Sample answer:**

> In one project, I had to work with a technology I hadn't used before, and we had a relatively short deadline.
>
> Instead of trying to learn the entire framework, I first identified exactly which parts were required for the feature I needed to build.
>
> I started with the official documentation and a small proof of concept. Once I understood the basic concepts, I implemented a small part of the feature and validated that my approach worked.
>
> Then I gradually expanded the implementation while checking the documentation whenever I encountered something unfamiliar.
>
> I also discussed the architecture with a teammate who had some experience with the technology.
>
> This approach allowed me to learn only what was necessary for the immediate problem while still building a proper foundation for future work.
>
> The experience taught me that under a tight deadline, **focused learning is more effective than trying to master the entire technology first**.

### My learning approach

**Understand fundamentals → Build small POC → Implement real feature → Get feedback → Improve**
