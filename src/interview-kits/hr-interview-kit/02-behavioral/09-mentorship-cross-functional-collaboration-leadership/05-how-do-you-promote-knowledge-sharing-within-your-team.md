# How do you promote knowledge sharing within your team?

**Definition:**  
This evaluates whether you help create a team where knowledge is shared instead of being concentrated in one or two people.

**Sample answer:**

> I prefer lightweight and practical knowledge sharing rather than meetings that exist only for the sake of meetings.
>
> For example, if I solve an interesting technical problem, I document the solution and the reasoning behind it. If the topic is useful to the team, I can also do a short walkthrough during a team meeting.
>
> I also encourage developers to explain their decisions during code reviews instead of only discussing whether the code works.
>
> Another practice I find useful is short technical sessions where team members explain something they've recently learned or implemented.
>
> I also believe documentation should live close to the code or project where possible, so people can find it when they actually need it.
>
> The goal is to make knowledge accessible to everyone rather than making one person the only expert on a particular area.
