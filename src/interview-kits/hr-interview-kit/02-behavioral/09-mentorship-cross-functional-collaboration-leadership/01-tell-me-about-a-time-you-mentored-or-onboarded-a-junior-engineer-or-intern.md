# Tell me about a time you mentored or onboarded a junior engineer or intern.

**Definition:**  
This evaluates whether you can transfer knowledge, provide guidance, and help someone become productive without doing all the work for them.

**Sample answer:**

> In one project, I helped onboard a junior developer who was new to our React codebase. Instead of explaining the entire application at once, I first gave them an overview of the architecture, folder structure, and development workflow.
>
> I then assigned a small, well-defined task so they could understand the codebase through practical work. I reviewed their PRs and explained not only what needed to change but also why.
>
> Whenever they were stuck, I encouraged them to investigate first and then discuss their approach with me. This helped them become more independent rather than relying on me for every decision.
>
> After a few weeks, they were able to pick up features independently and contribute confidently.
>
> I learned that good mentoring is less about giving someone the answers and more about helping them develop the ability to find those answers themselves.

**Good mentoring approach:**

> **Explain → Demonstrate → Let them try → Review → Give feedback → Increase ownership**
