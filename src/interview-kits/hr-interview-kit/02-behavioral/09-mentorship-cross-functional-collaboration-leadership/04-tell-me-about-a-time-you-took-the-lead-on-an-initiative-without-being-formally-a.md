# Tell me about a time you took the lead on an initiative without being formally asked.

**Definition:**  
This evaluates **initiative and ownership**. The interviewer wants evidence that you identify problems and act on them instead of waiting for someone to assign every task.

**Sample answer:**

> In one project, I noticed that developers were repeatedly spending time investigating the same issues because our internal documentation wasn't clear.
>
> It wasn't part of my assigned sprint work, but I noticed the repeated pattern and decided to improve it.
>
> I created a short developer guide covering the project setup, important architectural decisions, common debugging steps, and API conventions.
>
> I also asked the team to review it and incorporated their feedback.
>
> After that, onboarding and troubleshooting became easier because developers had a common reference point.
>
> I didn't consider it a separate project. I saw it as a small improvement that could remove repeated friction for the entire team.

**Strong leadership principle:**

> **Leadership isn't always about having authority. It's often about noticing a problem and taking ownership of improving it.**
