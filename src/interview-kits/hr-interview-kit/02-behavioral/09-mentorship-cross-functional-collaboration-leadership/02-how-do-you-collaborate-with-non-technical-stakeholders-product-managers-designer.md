# How do you collaborate with non-technical stakeholders (Product Managers, Designers, QA, Sales)?

**Definition:**  
This tests whether you can work effectively with people who have different responsibilities, priorities, and technical knowledge.

**Sample answer:**

> I try to communicate based on the other person's role rather than using technical terminology unnecessarily.
>
> With Product Managers, I focus on requirements, scope, priorities, and business impact. With Designers, I discuss user experience, states, responsiveness, and implementation constraints. With QA, I focus on expected behavior, edge cases, and reproducibility of issues.
>
> For example, if a Product Manager asks for a feature that has significant technical complexity, I don't simply say that it's difficult. I explain what makes it complex, what the impact is, and what simpler alternatives we could consider.
>
> I also try to involve stakeholders early when there are ambiguities instead of making assumptions and discovering problems later.
>
> My goal is to make sure everyone has the same understanding of what we're building and why we're building it.

### Simple communication model

```text
Product → What & Why
Design  → How it should behave
Engineering → How it can be built
QA → How it should be validated
Sales → Customer/business expectations
```
