# Give an example of when you went above and beyond your defined job description.

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to understand whether you take ownership of problems outside your immediate task boundaries when doing so helps the team or product.

**Sample answer:**

> In one project, my primary responsibility was implementing frontend features. During development, I noticed that several frontend issues were actually caused by unclear API contracts between the frontend and backend.
>
> Although API design wasn't directly my responsibility, I worked with the backend developer to document the expected request and response structures and identify a few inconsistencies.
>
> I also created a small API contract example that the team could use during development.
>
> This reduced back-and-forth between the frontend and backend teams and made integration smoother.
>
> I don't think going above and beyond means taking over someone else's responsibilities. For me, it means taking ownership when I see a problem that is affecting the team's ability to deliver.

**Important:**  
Don't make the answer sound like:

> "I regularly work outside my role and do everyone's job."

Instead, show **initiative + boundaries + team impact**.
