# Tell me about a time a project was at risk of missing a strict deadline. What actions did you take?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to know whether you can recognize delivery risks early, prioritize effectively, and coordinate with others when deadlines are tight.

**Sample answer:**

> In one project, we were approaching a release deadline and several tasks were still incomplete. I noticed that some of the remaining work was dependent on each other, so simply working longer hours wouldn't solve the problem.
>
> I first identified the critical-path tasks and separated them from nice-to-have improvements. I discussed the situation with the team and suggested moving some non-critical items to a later release.
>
> I also took ownership of one of the blocked tasks and worked closely with the backend developer to resolve the dependency quickly. We tracked the remaining work closely and communicated the updated scope to the stakeholders.
>
> We were able to deliver the critical functionality within the deadline, while the lower-priority improvements were moved to the next sprint.
>
> The main lesson for me was that meeting a deadline isn't always about doing more work. It's about **prioritizing the right work early**.

**Key points:**  
`Identify risk → Find critical path → Reduce scope → Remove blockers → Communicate`
