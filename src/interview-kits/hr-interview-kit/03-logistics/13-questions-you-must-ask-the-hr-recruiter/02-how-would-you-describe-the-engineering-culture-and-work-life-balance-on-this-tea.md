# "How would you describe the engineering culture and work-life balance on this team?"

**Definition:**  
**Engineering culture** refers to how developers and engineers collaborate, communicate, review code, make technical decisions, and handle problems.

**Work-life balance** refers to how the team manages working hours, deadlines, on-call responsibilities, overtime, and personal time.

### Sample question

> “How would you describe the engineering culture on the team? I'm particularly interested in how the team approaches code reviews, technical discussions, collaboration, and work-life balance.”

### Good follow-up questions

You can make the question more specific:

> “How often does the team have code reviews or design discussions?”

or:

> “Are there regular periods where the team needs to work beyond normal hours because of releases or production issues?”

This can give you more concrete information than simply asking:

> “Do you have good work-life balance?”
