# "What does success look like for this role in the first 90 days?"

**Definition:**  
This question asks the interviewer to explain what they expect from you during your **first three months**.

It helps you understand:

- What you are expected to learn
- What projects you may work on
- Which responsibilities are important
- How your performance will be evaluated
- What outcomes the team considers successful

### Sample question

> “What would success look like for someone in this role during the first 90 days? Are there specific technical or project milestones you'd expect them to achieve?”

### Why it's useful

Instead of asking only about the job description, you're trying to understand the **actual expectations after joining**.
