# "What are the biggest challenges the team is currently facing?"

**Definition:**  
This question helps you understand the team's **current technical, product, or organizational problems**.

For an engineering role, challenges might involve:

- Technical debt
- Scaling problems
- Legacy systems
- Performance issues
- Deployment complexity
- Testing gaps
- Tight delivery timelines
- Large codebases
- Communication between teams

### Sample question

> “What are the biggest technical or product challenges the team is currently working through?”

### Strong follow-up

If the interviewer mentions a challenge:

> “That's interesting. What have you tried so far to address it, and what would you expect the person joining this role to contribute?”

This turns the conversation into a discussion about the **actual engineering problems** you'll be working on.
