# "What are the opportunities for mentorship, learning, and career advancement?"

**Definition:**  
This question explores how the company supports your **professional and technical growth**.

You can ask about:

- Mentorship
- Senior engineers
- Technical training
- Conference or course budgets
- Architecture/design opportunities
- Ownership of projects
- Promotion criteria
- Engineering career paths
- Individual development plans

### Sample question

> “How does the team support engineers who want to grow technically? Are there opportunities for mentorship, taking ownership of projects, or progressing toward senior engineering responsibilities?”

### For a React/frontend developer

You could make it more specific:

> “As a frontend engineer, would there be opportunities to contribute beyond feature development, such as frontend architecture, performance optimization, design systems, or technical decision-making?”

That gives you a better understanding of whether the role offers **technical ownership** rather than only ticket-based development.
