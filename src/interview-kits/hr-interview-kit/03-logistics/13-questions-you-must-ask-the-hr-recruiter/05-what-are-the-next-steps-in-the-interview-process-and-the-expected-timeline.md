# "What are the next steps in the interview process and the expected timeline?"

**Definition:**  
This question asks the interviewer to explain **what happens after the current interview** and approximately when you can expect the next update.

The process could include:

```text
Current interview
      ↓
Technical round
      ↓
Manager / behavioral round
      ↓
HR discussion
      ↓
Offer
      ↓
Background verification
      ↓
Joining
```

Not every company follows this exact process.

### Sample question

> “Could you walk me through the remaining steps in the interview process and the expected timeline for the next stage?”

### If you've completed the final round

> “Since this is the final round, could you share what the remaining steps are and when I might expect to hear back?”

This is professional and completely reasonable to ask.
