# What is your official notice period?

**Definition:**  
Your **notice period** is the amount of time you are contractually required to work after resigning before your employment officially ends.

For example, if your company has a **60-day notice period**, you normally need to work for 60 days after submitting your resignation.

### Sample answer

> “My official notice period is 60 days. I would need to complete the required notice period unless my current employer approves an earlier release.”

### If you are already serving notice

> “My official notice period is 60 days, and I’ve already started serving it. My last working day is expected to be November 25.”
