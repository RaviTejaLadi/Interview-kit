# What is your earliest possible joining date?

**Definition:**  
This asks for the **earliest realistic date** on which you can officially start working for the new company.

Your answer should consider:

- Notice period
- Early-release possibility
- Buyout
- Existing commitments
- Background verification
- Relocation, if applicable

### Sample answer — currently employed

> “My official notice period is 60 days, so my normal joining date would be approximately 60 days after resignation. I can explore an early release, and if that's approved, I may be able to join earlier.”

### Sample answer — already serving notice

> “I'm currently serving my notice period, and my last working day is October 15. So October 16 would be my earliest possible joining date.”

### Sample answer — immediately available

> “I'm currently available to join immediately, so I can start based on your onboarding schedule.”
