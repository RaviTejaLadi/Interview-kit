# Do you have any planned vacations or commitments in the coming months?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to know whether you have any **pre-planned leave or commitments** that could affect onboarding, training, or project timelines.

You should be honest, especially if the absence is already planned.

### If you have no plans

> “I don't currently have any planned vacations or commitments that would affect my availability.”

### If you have a planned vacation

> “Yes, I have a pre-planned vacation from December 10 to December 15. Apart from those dates, I don't have any other planned commitments that would affect my availability.”

### If the vacation is not yet booked

> “I don't have any confirmed vacations at the moment. I may have some personal commitments later in the year, but nothing that would significantly affect my availability.”
