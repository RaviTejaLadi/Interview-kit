# Can your notice period be bought out or negotiated for an early release?

**Definition:**  
**Notice period buyout** means paying an amount equivalent to some or all of the remaining notice period so that you can leave earlier.

An **early release** means your current employer agrees to reduce the notice period without necessarily requiring a full buyout.

The exact rules depend on your employment contract and company policy.

### Sample answer

> “Yes, there is a possibility of notice-period buyout, subject to my company's policy and approval. I can also discuss an early release with my manager and HR if required.”

### If buyout isn't available

> “My company generally requires employees to complete the notice period, but I can discuss an early release with my manager and HR. I don't want to commit to an earlier date until I have confirmation.”

This is better than promising:

> “I can definitely join in two weeks.”

unless you already have confirmation.
