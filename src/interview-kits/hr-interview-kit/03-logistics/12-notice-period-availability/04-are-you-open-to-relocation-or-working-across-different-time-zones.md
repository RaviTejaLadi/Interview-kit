# Are you open to relocation or working across different time zones?

**Definition:**  
This question checks your **geographical and scheduling flexibility**.

There are two separate things here:

- **Relocation** → moving to another city/country for the job.
- **Time-zone flexibility** → occasionally or regularly working with teams in different time zones.

You don't need to say yes to everything. Give a clear answer based on your actual availability.

### Sample answer — flexible

> “Yes, I'm open to relocation if the role requires it. I'm also comfortable collaborating with teams across different time zones. I'm flexible with occasional overlap hours as long as the schedule is communicated in advance.”

### Sample answer — remote preferred

> “I'm comfortable working with teams across different time zones, particularly for scheduled meetings and collaboration. For relocation, I'd prefer a remote or hybrid arrangement, but I'm open to discussing the requirements of the role.”

### Good point to clarify

If the company says **“US time-zone overlap”**, ask:

> “Could you clarify how many hours of overlap are expected and whether those hours are consistent every day?”

That's more useful than simply saying yes.
