# Are you open to negotiation, or is this number strict?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to know how flexible your compensation expectations are.

Generally, it's better to show **reasonable flexibility** rather than presenting your first number as an absolute demand.

### Sample answer

> “Yes, I’m open to discussing it. The range I mentioned reflects my current expectations, but I’m willing to consider the complete compensation package, including fixed compensation, variable pay, equity, benefits, and the scope of the role.”

### If you have a minimum

You can still be flexible without giving away your bottom line:

> “I’m open to negotiation, although I’d like the final package to be aligned with the responsibilities of the position and my experience.”

### Avoid

> “I’ll accept whatever you offer.”

You don't want to communicate that you have no compensation expectations.
