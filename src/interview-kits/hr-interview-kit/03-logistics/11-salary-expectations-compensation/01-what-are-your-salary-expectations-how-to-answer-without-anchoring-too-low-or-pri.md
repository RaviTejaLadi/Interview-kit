# "What are your salary expectations?" (How to answer without anchoring too low or pricing yourself out).

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to understand whether your compensation expectations fit the role's budget and your experience level.

### How to answer

Avoid giving a single number too early. A **range** gives you room to negotiate.

A good structure is:

> “Based on my experience, technical skills, and the responsibilities of this role, I’m looking for something in the range of ₹X–₹Y LPA. However, I’m flexible depending on the overall compensation package and the scope of the role.”

### Sample answer

> “Based on my experience with React, JavaScript, TypeScript, and frontend architecture, I’m targeting around ₹18–22 LPA. That said, I’m open to discussing the overall package depending on the responsibilities, growth opportunities, and benefits.”

### 💡 Tip

Don't say:

> “Anything is fine.”

It can make your expectations unclear and may anchor the negotiation too low.

Also avoid:

> “I need at least ₹25 LPA.”

unless you're prepared to walk away below that amount.
