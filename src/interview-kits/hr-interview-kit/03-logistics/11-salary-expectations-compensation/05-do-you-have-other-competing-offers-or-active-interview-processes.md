# Do you have other competing offers or active interview processes?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer wants to understand your **job-search status and timeline**. It can also help them understand whether they need to move quickly with the process.

You should be honest. You don't need to reveal company names or exact offer amounts unless you want to.

### If you have active interviews

> “Yes, I’m currently in a couple of interview processes, although I’m still evaluating my options. My priority is finding a role where the technical responsibilities, team, and compensation are aligned.”

### If you have an offer

> “Yes, I currently have an offer, and I'm evaluating it alongside other opportunities. I’m particularly interested in understanding the role, technical challenges, and overall compensation package before making a final decision.”

### If you don't have other offers

Don't try to manufacture competition.

> “I’m currently exploring a few opportunities, but I don’t have another offer at the moment. I’m focusing on finding the right role rather than applying purely based on compensation.”

That's completely reasonable.

# 🧠 A strong salary discussion flow

You can think about the conversation like this:

```text
Current compensation
        ↓
Expected compensation
        ↓
Role responsibilities
        ↓
Fixed + Variable + Equity + Benefits
        ↓
Negotiation
        ↓
Final package
```

### Example complete conversation

**Interviewer:** What are your salary expectations?

**You:**

> “Based on my experience and the responsibilities of this role, I'm targeting around ₹18–22 LPA. I'm open to discussing the exact number depending on the overall compensation structure.”

**Interviewer:** What's your current CTC?

**You:**

> “My current CTC is ₹15.6 LPA, with the majority as fixed compensation and a variable component based on performance.”

**Interviewer:** Is ₹22 LPA your minimum?

**You:**

> “No, ₹22 LPA is the upper end of my current expectation rather than a strict number. I'm open to discussing the overall package based on the role and responsibilities.”

**Interviewer:** Do you have other offers?

**You:**

> “I'm currently exploring a few opportunities, but I don't have another finalized offer at the moment. I'm primarily looking for a role where the technical scope, team, growth opportunities, and compensation are aligned.”

## Quick rules to remember

- **Give a range**, not an unnecessarily low single number.
- Don't lie about competing offers.
- Understand **fixed vs variable**.
- Don't treat the headline CTC as guaranteed money.
- Evaluate **equity based on vesting and liquidity**, not just its quoted value.
- Keep some flexibility during negotiation.
- Don't reveal your absolute minimum too early.
- Tie your expectations to **experience, skills, responsibilities, and market context**.
- Remember that compensation is only one part of evaluating an offer.
