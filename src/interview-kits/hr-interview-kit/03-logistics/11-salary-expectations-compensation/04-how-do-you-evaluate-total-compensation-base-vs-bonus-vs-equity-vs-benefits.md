# How do you evaluate total compensation (Base vs Bonus vs Equity vs Benefits)?

**Definition:**  
**Total compensation** is more than just your monthly salary. It includes everything you receive in exchange for your work.

A simple model is:

**Total Compensation = Base + Variable + Equity + Benefits**

| Component       | What it means                             |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Base            | Guaranteed annual salary                  |
| Bonus           | Performance-based compensation            |
| Equity          | ESOPs, RSUs, stock options, etc.          |
| Benefits        | Insurance, PF, gratuity, allowances, etc. |
| Joining bonus   | One-time payment                          |
| Retention bonus | Payment tied to staying for a period      |

### Example

Suppose two offers look like this:

**Offer A**

- Base: ₹18 LPA
- Bonus: ₹2 LPA
- Equity: ₹0
- Benefits: ₹1 LPA

**Offer B**

- Base: ₹15 LPA
- Bonus: ₹3 LPA
- Equity: ₹5 LPA
- Benefits: ₹1 LPA

The headline CTC alone doesn't tell you everything. You need to understand **how much is guaranteed, how much is performance-dependent, and how equity is valued and vested**.

### Sample interview answer

> “I look at the complete compensation package rather than only the headline CTC. I consider the fixed component first because it's guaranteed, then evaluate the variable component, equity value and vesting terms, benefits, and any one-time bonuses. I also consider the role's responsibilities and long-term growth opportunities.”

### Important equity questions

If a company offers ESOPs/RSUs, ask:

- How many shares/options?
- What is the exercise/strike price?
- What is the current valuation or share price?
- What is the vesting schedule?
- Is there a cliff?
- What happens if I leave the company?
- Is there a buyback or liquidity opportunity?
