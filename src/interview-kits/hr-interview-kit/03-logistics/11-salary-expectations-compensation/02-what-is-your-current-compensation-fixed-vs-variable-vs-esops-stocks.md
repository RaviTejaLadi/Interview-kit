# What is your current compensation (fixed vs variable vs ESOPs/stocks)?

**Definition:**  
The interviewer is asking about your existing **total compensation (CTC)** and how it is divided.

Usually, compensation can include:

- **Fixed/base salary** — guaranteed salary
- **Variable/bonus** — performance-based compensation
- **ESOPs/equity** — ownership or stock-related compensation
- **Benefits** — insurance, gratuity, PF, allowances, etc.

### Sample answer

> “My current total compensation is ₹15.6 LPA. The majority is fixed compensation, with a variable component based on performance. I also have standard benefits such as PF and insurance.”

If you have equity:

> “My current CTC is ₹18 LPA, consisting of ₹15 LPA fixed, ₹2 LPA performance-based variable compensation, and approximately ₹1 LPA in equity benefits.”

### If you don't want to disclose it

You can redirect the conversation toward the role:

> “I’d prefer to focus on the compensation range for this position and the value I can bring to the role. Based on my experience and the responsibilities involved, I’m targeting ₹X–Y LPA.”

That's professional and avoids unnecessarily anchoring the negotiation to your current salary.
