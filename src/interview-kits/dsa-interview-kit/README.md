# 📘 DSA Interview Questions

# 📚 PART 1 - THEORY QUESTIONS

---

## 1. DSA Fundamentals ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

1. How do you analyze time and space complexity?
2. What are the most common data structures and when should you use each?
3. How do you pick the right approach during an interview?

---

## 2. Arrays, Strings, and Hashing Patterns ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

1. When should you use the two-pointers pattern?
2. When should you use sliding window?
3. How do hash maps/sets and prefix sums optimize brute-force solutions?

---

## 3. Recursion, Backtracking, and Dynamic Programming ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

1. What is recursion and what are common pitfalls?
2. What is backtracking and how is it different from brute force?
3. How do you recognize dynamic programming problems quickly?

---

## 4. Trees, Graphs, and Heaps ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

1. Which tree traversals are must-know for interviews?
2. BFS vs DFS: when should you choose one over the other?
3. When is a heap (priority queue) the best fit?
