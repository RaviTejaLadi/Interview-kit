# How do you pick the right approach during an interview?

Use this sequence:

1. Clarify constraints (input size, sorted/unsorted, duplicates, memory limit).
2. Start with brute force to establish correctness.
3. Identify pattern (hashing, two pointers, sliding window, recursion, graph traversal, DP).
4. Optimize and explain why the new complexity is better.
5. Validate with edge cases.

## High-value communication

- Say your assumptions out loud.
- Call out complexity before writing full code.
- If stuck, discuss alternatives instead of going silent.
